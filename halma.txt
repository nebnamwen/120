== How to play Halma ==

How to run:
    python halma.py

Object:
    Move all your pieces to the same-colored home zone on the opposite
    side of the board, where your opponent's pieces began.

How to move:
    Move one piece, either a single slide to an adjacent space,
    or a series of jumps over other pieces (of either color).

How to interact:
    Click to select a piece.
    Click on a target space to move there.
    If the move was a jump, the piece remains selected and can jump again.
    End your turn by pressing P to switch players.

Ending the game:
    The game detects when a player wins and decorates their pieces with stars.
    Close the window to exit.
